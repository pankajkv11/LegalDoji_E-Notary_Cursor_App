## 🔄 User Journey

### For Notaries:
1. Visit `/join-notary` landing page
2. Click "Apply Now"
3. Fill form at `/notary/apply`
4. Upload Bar Council certificate
5. Submit application
6. Receive confirmation with Application ID
7. Wait for admin verification (24-48 hrs)
8. Get email notification
9. Access notary dashboard once approved

### For Users:
1. Visit `/how-it-works`
2. Understand the 3-step process
3. See pricing breakdown
4. Click "Create Document"
5. Follow the process:
   - Submit form with details
   - Choose delivery option
   - Get video notarization (optional)
